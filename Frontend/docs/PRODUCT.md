# Veyra — Product Strategy & Feature Roadmap

## 1. Product Vision

**Veyra** (Fermor) is an AI-first personal wealth and financial intelligence workspace designed to give individuals complete clarity over their financial life. Unlike traditional budgeting tools that require tedious manual entry, Veyra connects accounts, analyzes cash flows, detects pattern anomalies, and projects future net worth automatically.

---

## 2. Core Feature Specifications

### 📊 1. Money at a Glance (Dashboard)
* **Net Worth Hero Chart**: Interactive 6-month net worth trajectory visualization with period toggles (1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, ALL).
* **Asset & Debt Bento Grid**: Real-time breakdown of liquid cash, invested assets, and total debt utilization.
* **Monthly Split Allocation**: Income vs. expense vs. savings distribution bar displaying percentage breakdown and savings rate badges.

### 🤖 2. Contextual AI Observations ("Veyra Noticed")
* **Spending Anomaly Alert**: Detects when monthly spending exceeds historic averages and pinpoints exact category drivers (e.g. dining out, subscriptions).
* **Savings Opportunity**: Identifies excess idle cash and recommends automated transfers to high-yield savings or investment goals.
* **Health Score Influencers**: Explains specific deltas (+8 points savings rate, -3 debt utilization) influencing the user's score.

### 🎯 3. Goal Tracker & Wealth Simulator
* **Custom Goal Cards**: Track targets for emergency funds, home down payments, vacation savings, and retirement.
* **Interactive Projection Slider**: Dynamic slider enabling users to adjust monthly contributions and immediately visualize goal completion timelines.

### 🛡️ 4. Financial Health Score (0–100 Engine)
* **Comprehensive Health Rating**: Evaluates savings rate, debt-to-income ratio, emergency fund coverage months, and investment diversification.
* **Health Breakdown Page**: Deep-dive analytics detailing score history and recommended improvement actions.

### 🛠️ 5. Financial Tools & Explore Suite
* Built-in calculators for FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early), Mortgage & EMI comparisons, Tax estimates, and Compound Interest modeling.

### 💬 6. Floating AI Assistant ("Ask Veyra")
* Contextual floating assistant accessible across all screens via floating action button or `Ctrl+J` shortcut.
* Answers complex financial queries (*"Can I afford a $1,200 purchase this month?"*, *"How much should I save for retirement?"*).
