# Tooltip Rules

## Purpose

Tooltips explain unfamiliar concepts without repeating visible text.

## Good Tooltip

Financial Health

"Your score reflects your savings, spending, investments, and other financial factors."

## Bad Tooltip

Financial Health

"Financial Health"

## Rules

Tooltips should:

- Explain
- Clarify
- Add useful context
- Remain concise

Tooltips should not:

- Repeat the label
- Contain large paragraphs
- Hide essential information
- Replace accessible visible instructions

## Financial Definitions

Use tooltips for concepts that may require explanation.

Examples:

Net Worth
Financial Health
Estimated completion
Available balance

## Data Freshness

A tooltip can explain what a timestamp means.

Example:

"Last successful sync with your connected account."

## AI

Do not use tooltips to hide important AI limitations.

Important limitations should be visible.
