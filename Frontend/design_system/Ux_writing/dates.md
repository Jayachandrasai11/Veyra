# Date and Time Rules

## Purpose

Make financial data freshness easy to understand.

## Relative Time

Use relative time for dashboard scanning.

Examples:

Updated today

Updated 2 hours ago

Updated yesterday

Updated 12 hours ago

## Exact Time

Use exact timestamps in detailed views when useful.

Example:

Updated Aug 22, 2026 at 10:42 AM

## Data Freshness

Always communicate freshness when it materially affects interpretation.

Example:

Net Worth
₹18.4L
Updated 12 hours ago

## Future Dates

Use explicit dates for financial goals.

Example:

Estimated completion
March 2027

## Avoid Ambiguity

Avoid:

"Recently"

"Some time ago"

"Later"

when exact or relative timing is available.

## Time Zones

Display times in the user's relevant local timezone unless the context requires another timezone.

## Consistency

Do not mix several date formats arbitrarily across the product.
