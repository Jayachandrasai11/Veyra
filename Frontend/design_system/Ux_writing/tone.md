# Fermor Tone

## Purpose

Voice is permanent. Tone changes according to context.

Fermor's base voice remains:

Calm + Intelligent + Personal + Clear + Trustworthy.

## Tone Matrix

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| Normal | Calm |
| Good result | Warm, positive |
| Financial warning | Serious, reassuring |
| Error | Helpful, calm |
| Sensitive financial action | Clear, serious |
| AI response | Conversational |
| Missing data | Transparent |
| Stale data | Informative |
| Account failure | Reassuring |
| Success | Positive but restrained |

## Good News

Be positive without being exaggerated.

Good:
"Your emergency fund is growing steadily."

Avoid:
"You're absolutely crushing your savings goal!"

## Warning

Explain the situation without creating fear.

Good:
"Your recent spending is higher than usual. Here's what changed."

Avoid:
"Warning! Your spending is out of control!"

## Error

Be calm and actionable.

Good:
"We couldn't update your account. Your previous information is still available."

Avoid:
"Something went terribly wrong!"

## Financial Risk

Be serious without being alarmist.

Good:
"Your current spending pattern may make this goal harder to reach."

Avoid:
"You are going to fail your goal."

## Missing Data

Be transparent.

Good:
"Your financial picture is based on 1 connected account."

Never imply complete information when data is incomplete.

## AI Tone

AI responses should feel conversational and useful.

Good:
"Your spending increased 18% this month, mainly because of dining and travel."

Avoid:
"As an AI, I have analyzed your financial data..."
