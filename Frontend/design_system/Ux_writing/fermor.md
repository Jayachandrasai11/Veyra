# 08.1 — Fermor Voice

## Purpose

Define the writing and communication style used throughout Fermor.

The UI, static content, generated insights, notifications, errors, recommendations, and AI responses must all feel like the same product.

---

## Fermor Personality

Fermor should feel:

- Calm
- Intelligent
- Personal
- Clear
- Trustworthy
- Helpful
- Non-judgmental

Fermor should feel like a thoughtful financial companion.

It should not feel like:

- A traditional banking portal
- A financial sales platform
- A generic productivity dashboard
- A chatbot wrapper
- A generic AI assistant

---

## Core Voice Rules

### 01. Calm

Use calm, factual language.

Preferred:

> Your spending increased 18% this month.

Avoid:

> ⚠ Your spending is dangerously high!

---

### 02. Intelligent

Show understanding without unnecessary technical language.

Preferred:

> Most of the increase came from dining and travel.

Avoid:

> Our AI detected anomalous expenditure patterns.

---

### 03. Personal

Speak directly to the user and their financial context.

Preferred:

> Your emergency fund is growing steadily.

Avoid:

> Users with similar financial profiles typically...

---

### 04. Clear

Say what happened and what the user can do next.

Preferred:

> Your investment account hasn't synced since yesterday.

Avoid:

> There appears to be a potential synchronization issue.

---

### 05. Trustworthy

Never claim more certainty than the available data supports.

Use:

- Based on your connected accounts...
- We estimate...
- Your available data shows...
- Updated 12 hours ago...
- Your financial picture is incomplete...

Avoid:

- You will definitely...
- Fermor knows...
- This will guarantee...
- You cannot afford...

---
