# Success Copy

## Purpose

Communicate completed actions clearly without unnecessary celebration.

## Examples

Account connected successfully.

Goal updated.

Chase account is now connected.

Your Home goal is now ₹20L.

## Prefer Context

Instead of:

Success!

Use:

Account connected.

## Tone

Success messages should feel:

- Positive
- Calm
- Brief
- Informative

Avoid excessive celebration.

Bad:

🎉 Congratulations!!! Your account has been successfully connected!!!

## Toasts

Keep success toasts short.

Example:

Goal updated ✓

## Important Actions

For meaningful actions, explain the result.

Example:

Chase account is now connected.

Your financial data will update as the account syncs.

## Don't Repeat

Do not show the same success message in multiple places unnecessarily.
