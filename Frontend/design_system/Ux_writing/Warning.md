# Warning Copy

## Purpose

Warnings communicate a condition that requires attention but does not necessarily mean the system has failed.

## Tone

Warnings are:

- Calm
- Informative
- Specific
- Actionable

Never use alarmist language.

## Examples

Your investment account hasn't synced recently.

Your goal may be behind schedule based on your current contribution rate.

Your recent spending is higher than usual.

Your financial picture is based on 1 connected account.

## Warning Structure

Condition
→ Context
→ Optional action

Example:

Your investment data is from yesterday.

[Reconnect account]

## Warning vs Error

Warning:
The system is functioning but attention may be useful.

Error:
The intended operation failed.

Do not label a warning as an error.

## Color

Do not rely only on yellow/orange/red color.

The message itself must communicate the meaning.
