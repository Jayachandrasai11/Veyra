# Heading Rules

## Purpose

Create a consistent information hierarchy.

## Heading Hierarchy

### Page Heading

Used once for the main page identity.

Example:

"Here's your financial picture."

### Section Heading

Examples:

YOUR MONEY AT A GLANCE
FERMOR NOTICED
YOUR GOALS
EXPLORE

### Subsection Heading

Examples:

Financial Health
Net Worth
Investments
Emergency Fund

### Supporting Text

Examples:

↑ 6.2% this month
3 months covered
₹8L / ₹20L
Updated 2 hours ago

## Rules

Do not create a new heading style for every section.

Use hierarchy consistently.

Do not use:

- Excessively long headings
- Marketing slogans
- Technical terminology
- Multiple competing headings

## Sentence Case

Normal interface headings should generally use sentence case unless the established visual design specifically defines uppercase section labels.

Examples:

"Financial Health"

"Net Worth"

"Your goals"

## Section Labels

Existing Fermor editorial section labels may use:

YOUR MONEY AT A GLANCE
FERMOR NOTICED
YOUR GOALS

Do not randomly mix capitalization styles.
